Few things bring me as much joy as a genuinely delicious meal. I'm always on the hunt for the next great dish, any cuisine, any occasion. A few places I keep going back to:

Min's Kitchen

La Cañada · Order: Everything

My favorite is ordering family style and making a bowl of fried rice, pad see ew, satay, and sizzling beef, topped with cashews.

Taco Stand

San Diego · Order: Mar y Tierra Burrito

Marinated shrimp pairs excellently with their beef. All the toppings add the perfect amount of flavor and texture.

Silverlake Ramen

Southern California, with locations across the U.S. · Order: The Classic Ramen

Warm, soothing, and tasty on all soup levels.
