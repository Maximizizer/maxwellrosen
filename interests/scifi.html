Whether it's a novel or a film, I'm drawn to stories that ask "what if?" Sci-fi fuels my curiosity about the future of technology, society, and what it means to be human.

My Letterboxd ↗

Film

  • The Martian (2015): An insight into the wittiness of engineering to stay alive.
  • Project Hail Mary (2026): An emotional adventure to save humanity.

TV

  • For All Mankind (2019–): Encapsulates the fantasy of changing science history.

Books

  • Ender's Game (Orson Scott Card): A test to see if a child can carry the weight of human- and alien-kind on his shoulders.
  • The Martian (Andy Weir): Better than the movie!
  • Project Hail Mary (Andy Weir): Even better than the movie!
